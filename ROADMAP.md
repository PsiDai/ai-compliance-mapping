# Roadmap

Dates are intentionally not promised yet. Order is the intent.

1. **Structure** - control schema, framework identifiers, evidence fields.
2. **Draft mapping** - first controls mapped across EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001.
3. **Review** - check the mapping against the source texts; record gaps and judgement calls.
4. **v1 release** - versioned data, change log, licence decided.
5. **Later** - additional frameworks (Singapore, California are candidates).

Progress is also summarised at https://alchemy.consulting/ai-compliance-mapping
